# Music scene artwork

Asset: `site/music/retro-objects.png`.

The built-in image generation tool made this transparent still life for the
landing page. It shows a mix CD, clear case, and wired earbuds. The page
places a separate Blender render of the accurate Pixel 2 beside it. The
device screen comes from the real launcher.

Prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Create a single photorealistic transparent-background cutout asset for a premium retro handheld website. Subject: a nostalgic early-2000s personal music still life, with a clear slim CD jewel case, one silver CD-R partly slid out, a pale paper insert, and a pair of simple silver-and-white wired earbuds with a loose naturally tangled white cable. The CD has small believable handwritten dark marker text 'summer 04'. No other text. No logos, no brand names. No music player, no phone, no console, no hands. The real accurate handheld will be composited separately in the website. Composition: a carefully art-directed compact arrangement in a square canvas, photographed almost straight down with a slight natural angle. Jewel case rotated slightly clockwise and placed lower right; earbuds and loose cable curve toward the top left. Entire objects and cable must fit inside canvas with generous clean transparent margins. Materials: tiny real scratches on clear polycarbonate, restrained iridescent rainbow on the CD, soft plastic earbud details, warm subtle soft contact shadows with transparent falloff. Mood: a beloved pocket music collection from 2004, tactile and authentic, premium editorial product photography. Neutral soft studio lighting. Real transparency, no solid background, no checkerboard, no drop-in fake player screen, no gradients filling the empty background. The purpose is to accompany a green GKD Pixel 2 with its music screen.
