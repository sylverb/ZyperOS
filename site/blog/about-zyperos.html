ZyperOS is free software for the GKD Pixel 2. It brings games, music, books and videos to one small handheld.

The name

I named ZyperOS after Zyper, my miniature schnauzer, who passed away. He was quick, light on his feet and full of energy. I keep his memory in the name and artwork.

Source and community

The source repository contains the code and development history. Visit the ZyperOS Discord for questions, ideas and discussion. Report a software problem through the issue tracker.

For everyday use, start with the ZyperOS guide. The installation guide explains the installer and card image.

Built on open source

Many projects make ZyperOS possible. I wrote a thank-you to Eric Reinsmidt about the work I built on.

  • Eric Reinsmidt's TortOS, its installer, plastron and Diatom provided the starting point.
  • ROCKNIX supplied kernel patches and the Pixel 2 device tree.
  • PortMaster supplies ports and their runtimes.
  • plorpOS, ROGUE OS, libretro authors, OpenBOR and PPSSPP contribute emulation and game support.
  • Deadstation Portable supplies ARM64 improvements for PSP.
  • PrBoom, Gearcoleco and TIC-80 support their respective shelves.
  • Sharp-Shimmerless and Timothy Lottes' CRT shader provide display options.

The complete notices identify included software and licenses.

Music

ZyperOS includes ten menu tracks from OpenGameArt composers under CC0. The landing page previews three of them.

  • Starfall Island by John Oestmann, from minimal_worlds_ii.
  • Napping on a Cloud by congusbongus.
  • Bossa Nova by Joth.

The preview sleeves are original website illustrations. They are not the artists' official album covers. The music guide explains how to add your own albums.

Support
