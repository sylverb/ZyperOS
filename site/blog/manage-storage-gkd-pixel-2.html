ZyperOS Storage shows what fills the card and how much space each game uses. It also lists music, videos and audiobooks. Open it from the home screen at MENU > System > Storage.

These steps cover ZyperOS 1.0.5. Check your version in MENU > About.

Read the storage bar

The top shows used space and free space. The colored bar divides the card into six parts. The first scan measures the files; later visits show stored sizes while the scan updates them.

PartContents
GamesGame files, without their artwork
SavesGame saves and save states
ArtCovers and video previews
MediaMusic, videos, audiobooks and menu music
ZyperOSZyperOS and emulator files
OtherBIOS files, settings and other card data

Find large games or media

  1. Select a system and press A.
  2. Check each game's size and the details below the list.
  3. Press X to change the order.
  4. Press B to return to the card list.

Size puts the largest first. A to Z sorts by name. Last Played puts recent games first. Each list remembers its order.

Open Music, Videos or Audiobooks to inspect albums, films and books. These lists offer Size and A to Z. They have no Last Played order.

Remove unused art or old states

Storage shows cleanup choices when they can recover at least 10 MB.

  • Unused Art removes covers and video previews for games no longer on the shelf.
  • Old Save States removes a game's states only when no state changed and no play occurred for a year.

This also includes old states for games no longer on the card. Old Save States keeps game saves. It removes the separate emulator states. A system's Delete Video Snaps removes its video previews while keeping its games.

Delete a game, album or video

Back up files you want to keep before deletion. Use the File Transfer page, or copy them with a card reader.

  1. Select the item in Storage and press A.
  2. Read the name, size and deletion message.
  3. Choose Delete only if you want to remove that item.

The question starts on Cancel. B also cancels. Deletion cannot be undone.

Delete Game removes its files, saves, states, cover and video preview. It also removes local details and play-time records. Files that another game still uses stay.

An album or book folder goes with its contents. A video series folder goes with its videos. The displayed size helps you compare choices before deletion.
