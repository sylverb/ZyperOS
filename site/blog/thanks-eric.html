I am very grateful to Eric Reinsmidt. His work on TortOS motivated me to finally build ZyperOS. I forked his project, and I want that credit to be easy to find.

Why I am grateful

I wanted to build an OS for the Pixel 2 for a long time. The existing interfaces never felt right to me. TortOS gave me the inspiration and the starting point to take that first step.

My background is in product and user experience. I love building things that help people reach their goals without the interface getting in their way.

Eric may not like me after our disagreements about ZyperOS. I can understand that. I am still very grateful to him for the opportunity and for his work in this small community.

I respect the focused direction he chose for TortOS. I have a broader vision for ZyperOS. I still value the work that made my first step possible.

The fast boot

Thanks, Eric! The fast boot comes from your work on TortOS, which I forked to build ZyperOS.

That speed is a large part of how the handheld feels. I can put it down, turn it off, and return to a game quickly. My changes build on that starting point.

More than the launcher

Eric's work reaches several parts of ZyperOS. I want to name them clearly.

Eric's projectWhat I built on
TortOSThe starting point for the ZyperOS launcher and its fast start.
TortOS InstallerThe installer, including its disk writer, card detection, and disk access on each computer platform.
plastronThe Linux system and build that run underneath ZyperOS.
DiatomThe emulator frontend that runs the game cores.

The installer

My installer started with Eric's TortOS Installer. Its disk writer, card listing, and platform-specific disk access provided the starting point.

I changed its interface and connected it to ZyperOS releases. Eric's installer remains the starting point for that work.

Keeping the credit visible

I keep Eric's copyright notices with his code. This page adds a visible thank-you beside those notices.

Other projects and authors also contribute to ZyperOS. The project credits and complete notices name that work.

If you enjoy ZyperOS, take a look at TortOS too. Eric's work deserves a visit.

Thanks, Eric. Your work gave me the starting point for ZyperOS.
