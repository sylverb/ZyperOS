ZyperOS 1.0.6 is the first release for a second handheld, the TrimUI Brick. On both, it puts a grid of covers on every shelf and on the home, lets you pick which systems the home shows, and adds Undo Load, On-Screen Stats and achievements you download ahead of time. On the Pixel 2, it also adds Screen Colors and changes how the Pixel 2 runs its memory, the likely cause of a freeze one player had.

## Install

1. Download **ZyperOS Installer** for your computer below: macOS (`.zip`), Windows (`.exe`), or Linux (`.deb` or `.rpm`).
2. Put the microSD card in the computer, open the installer, and pick your handheld (the GKD Pixel 2 or the TrimUI Brick), the latest release and the card. **Fresh install** erases the card and puts ZyperOS on it.
3. Put the card in the handheld and turn it on. The first start sets the card up (about a minute), then asks for your language, and Zyper shows you each button.

For the Pixel 2, you can also write `ZyperOS-v1.0.6-pixel2.img.xz` with any disk image writer (Raspberry Pi Imager, balenaEtcher). Check it against `SHA256SUMS` first. The Brick needs the installer, which adds its closed files to the card (below).

## The TrimUI Brick

1.0.6 runs on the TrimUI Brick (TG3040), from its own microSD card. It never writes to the Brick's own storage: take the card out, and the Brick starts its own system, as before. Not the Brick Pro yet.

- **Its closed files, from the installer:** the Brick's graphics and Wi-Fi need closed files made for its chips (Imagination's GPU driver and XRadio's Wi-Fi firmware), which ZyperOS does not pass on. So the installer downloads them for you from where they are published, Creality's Sonic Pad SDK on GitHub, checks each one, and puts them only on your card.
- **Not on the Brick yet:** the consoles whose cores draw with the GPU (Dreamcast, NAOMI, Atomiswave, Nintendo 64, PSP and Saturn) are hidden; they come once those cores run on the Brick's GPU. PortMaster's ports, Flash Drive, Screen Colors, the old-screen shaders (CRT, CRT Curved, Scanlines and LCD Grid; Sharp-Shimmerless is there), NES Player 2, the charge limit and the Brick's lights are not there yet either, and Bluetooth is not tested on it.
- **Tested on one Brick so far:** a tester on the Discord ran it on a TrimUI Brick, which has one of the six screens a Brick can come with. The Brick Hammer runs the same image, but nobody has tried it yet. If you have one, or anything looks wrong, your logs on the Discord help a lot: File Transfer's **Download logs**, or the card's `.userdata/brick/logs` folder.
- **From a test kit:** **MENU > System Update** offers 1.0.6 on a test kit's card. It is the Brick's first update, so I'd love to hear how it went.

What's new below is next to 1.0.5 on the Pixel 2; on the Brick, all of it is new.

## What's new

- **Grid views:** **MENU > View** has a third choice, **Grid**: two rows of covers, each cell in the shape of the shelf's art, so tall boxes sit five across and SNES boxes three. **START** on a shelf now goes Covers, Grid, List. **MENU > Display > Home Screen** has **Grid** too: three rows of six systems, 18 at once. Paging slides whole rows in, with their covers. Asked for on Discord.
- **Pick the home's systems, and Recents:** **MENU > Display > Home Systems** turns each system on or off on the home, Favorites and Recents too. A system you turn off leaves the home, Random Game, Search and Favorites, and its games stay on the card. The new **Recents**, right after Favorites, holds the ten games you played last. Asked for on Discord.
- **Screen Colors:** the last row of **MENU > Display** opens a page with a color chart and six rows: **Look** (Standard, Vivid, Intense, Warm, Cool, Muted and Night), **Temperature**, **Tint**, **Contrast**, **Saturation** and **Gamma**. Moving through the looks shows each one on the whole screen, and **B** puts back what you had. The colors cover the shelf, every game and every port. **Standard** changes nothing, and every card starts on it. On the Pixel 2. Asked for on Discord.
- **Undo Load:** the in-game menu's Game tab has **Undo Load** right under Load. Every load, from the slot strip or with **SELECT+L1**, first keeps the game as it was, and **Undo Load** takes you back to it, also after you quit. Asked for on Discord, by a player who lost their progress to a load.
- **On-Screen Stats:** the in-game menu's Display tab has **On-Screen Stats**, a line over the game with what you pick: FPS, Speed, CPU temperature, load and clock, the battery and the time. **A** opens its page: the corner, the distance from the edge, a pill or a shadow, the opacity, the color and the size. Speed shows only when the game is off its normal speed.
- **Achievements, downloaded ahead of time:** **MENU > Achievements** opens a page that downloads every game's achievements, their badges and what you already earned to the card, so a game you never played with Wi-Fi has its achievements offline. Your account is under **Y**. Nintendo DS games now find theirs too, where RetroAchievements has a set. Asked for on Discord.
- **See what a setting does:** the Display list shows the mode under the cursor on the game's own picture, its paused frame in a game, with what the mode does under it. The Shader and Strength lists show a scene through each filter. The display modes have plain names: Integer is now Sharp Pixels, Stretch is Full Screen and Aspect is Original Shape. Your setting stays as it was.
- **Timing, Smooth or Exact:** the Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance, Nintendo DS and Genesis shelves have a **Timing** row in their menu. **Smooth**, where they now start, runs a game at the screen's own rate, so motion stays even (on the Pixel 2, 60.08 Hz, at most 0.59% fast). **Exact** runs it at the console's own rate, as before, and repeats a frame every few seconds.
- **A card for every game with no art:** a game with no cover gets a card of its own, in the system's color, with a pattern of its era, ZyperOS's art of the system in a corner, and the name. Once you play it, the card shows a soft frame of your game, which turns sharp when you land on it.
- **Darker, and your brightness from the start:** on the Pixel 2, two new, darker brightness steps at the bottom, for bed or a dark room; the brightest stays as it was. The boot animation, the first-start and update lines and the charging screen now light at your brightness, not the system's. Asked for on Discord.
- **Power Button, Press or Hold:** **MENU > System > Power Button** can be **Hold**: hold POWER for two seconds to save and turn off, with a bar that fills while you hold, and let go to cancel. **Press**, as before, is where it starts. Asked for on Discord.
- **Fewer freezes, I hope:** one player's Pixel 2 froze in the menus, half a minute to a minute after it started. The likely cause is how the memory changed speed, so it now changes only between two frames, as the Pixel 2's own firmware does, with the chip's logic voltage held up while the processor runs fast. My own Pixel 2 never froze, so I can't say it's gone for everyone. If yours still freezes, put a file named `steady-memory` at the top of the PX2BOOT drive: the memory then stays at full speed, for a little more battery.
- **A freeze leaves a report:** when the processor hangs, the handheld now restarts by itself within a minute and leaves `zyperos-crash.txt` on the PX2BOOT drive, which any computer opens, and a copy in File Transfer's **Download logs**. A freeze of the memory itself still needs you to hold POWER.
- **A card a power cut left dirty starts:** a card left dirty by a power cut, or with a damaged file, now starts by itself. Before, the handheld could stay on the boot animation, or turn off at every start, until a computer repaired the card. A database a power cut broke is set aside and a new one is made, and if the launcher stops five times in a row at the start, ZyperOS copies its own files again from the system, never your games, saves or settings.
- **PSP:** on my Pixel 2, MotorStorm: Arctic Edge runs at 19 frames a second, where it ran at 3, and God of War: Chains of Olympus's menu at 31, where it ran at 17. PSP games now save their resume point each time you quit and start from it; before, a quit often saved nothing and the game started over. The sound no longer cuts at a start or a load. MotorStorm was asked for on Discord.
- **Nintendo DS:** DSperate is now 3.4.1, which fixes New Super Mario Bros. freezing after the DS slept, Picross DS (Korean release) stopping on a white screen, Zoo Keeper's title screen and Electroplankton's Rec-Rec, and your resume points still load. Games that show the time, like Pokémon Black, read your local time, and it runs. **When Slow** in the DS shelf's menu can hold a slow game to a steady 30 frames a second. Fast forward now goes past 1x where a game is heavy, and the sound no longer cuts after a load.
- **SG-1000:** a shelf of its own, `Roms/SG-1000`, for `.sg` and `.zip`, on Genesis Plus GX, with no BIOS. Asked for on Discord, to play Lode Runner.
- **File Transfer, several at once:** on File Transfer's page, pick several games (a row's **Select**, Ctrl or Cmd with a click, Shift for a range, or a finger held on a row), then **Move to folder** or **Delete** them at once. Every move, drags too, can be undone from the notice after it. Asked for on Discord.
- **Uploads that wait for the Wi-Fi:** an upload that loses the Wi-Fi now waits and goes on from where it stopped as soon as the handheld is back, and **Try again** goes on with one that waited too long. `wifi.log` in **Download logs** also records the signal now, so a network that keeps dropping can be looked into. From a player's logs on Discord.
- **The video player, one view:** the video fills the width, and the controls float over its foot when you press a key and go 3 seconds later. **Y** shows or hides them.
- **Lists open on A:** a row with more than two choices, like Auto Off or a system's Core, opens its list on **A**, with a tick on the one you have. A row with two choices switches on **A**. Left and Right still step through them.
- **A cleaner look:** names, menus and bars are laid out on one grid, so a menu's edges fall on whole pixels, and every screen's keys stand at the same height. The SELECT and START hints are round keys marked SE and ST, as on the Pixel 2 itself, and the bars are a little thicker.
- **The Scraper without names:** **MENU > Scraper > Settings** has **Names and Details**. Turn it off, and a run fetches only the missing covers and videos, with no names, years or synopses. Asked for on Discord.
- **A port that won't start:** after a minute on its loading screen, a port says how to leave (hold MENU and START), and MENU opens the port's menu over it, with **Keep Waiting**, **Controls** and **Quit**. A port that hangs leaves its reason in **Download logs**. Reported on Discord.
- **Dreamcast:** a second Dreamcast, NAOMI or Atomiswave game no longer starts the first game again. Asked for on Discord.
- **Saturn:** quitting a Saturn game no longer crashes the emulator, and the game after one that crashed has its in-game menu again.
- **A wrong BIOS, or an empty game, said:** a BIOS file of the wrong size is named under the game, with the size it should be, where the game started to a black screen. A game file that is empty or cut short says so, instead of crashing the emulator.
- **PICO-8:** short taps reach the cart, and a held direction repeats. Asked for on Discord.
- **Doom:** **START > Quit Game** quits, each game starts on a fresh PrBoom (no more episodes of the last game), you run and **Y** walks, the d-pad turns at the original game's speed, and the rumble is softer. Asked for on Discord.
- **Quick save and quick load:** **SELECT+R1** and **SELECT+L1** now take a shoulder pressed a moment before SELECT, and one pressed after a long hold of SELECT. Reported on Discord.
- **After a game crashes:** the shelf is back on the screen within a second. Before, it could stay dark until a restart.
- **The battery level at a start:** after the handheld was off for a while, the battery level no longer starts too low, and on a charger no longer too high. A player saw 85% when they turned it off and 49% two hours later.
- **Quieter at the bottom:** the speaker's two lowest volume steps are quieter than the old lowest. Headphones stay as they were. Asked for on Discord.
- **Cores with their own fixes:** mGBA no longer buzzes under the music of the Classic NES Series games, Snes9x 2010's fades are no longer too bright (Axelay's sky), and Genesis Plus GX fixes Master System and Game Gear graphics and Soldiers of Fortune's title screen. Your saved states still load.
- **Favorites' Display row:** a game started from Favorites shows its own display mode in the in-game menu. Reported on Discord.
- **Developer Mode over Wi-Fi:** **MENU > Connections > Developer Mode** has **SSH over Wi-Fi**, off to start, which takes SSH keys only. SSH also stays up while a port runs, and comes back after the launcher restarts.
- **Logs that say more:** **Download logs** now names the device, its system and its hardware, and carries the kernel's log and each start's battery line.
- **About and the translations:** About's thanks are one page that scrolls. Words that didn't fit in German, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian now do, and two Spanish lines on the Scraper page read better.

## Updating from 1.0.5

On the Pixel 2, **MENU > System Update** offers 1.0.6 over Wi-Fi, the system and ZyperOS together, or use **Update** in ZyperOS Installer, which keeps your games, music and saves. Part of this release is in the system itself (the memory, the freeze report, the battery level, the boot brightness, Saturation), and both ways install it. From 1.0.3, File Transfer over the cable doesn't connect, so update over Wi-Fi or with the installer.

The Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance, Nintendo DS and Genesis shelves start on **Timing: Smooth**. For the console's own rate, pick **Exact** in the shelf's menu.

Your brightness moves once to the nearest of the new steps, so it looks about the same.

A Dreamcast game you started right after another one on 1.0.5 may have kept that other game as its resume point. Start it once with **Y** (From the Start).

Save states from 1.0.5 load in 1.0.6. If you go back to 1.0.5, some states saved in 1.0.6 don't load there, those of the SNES and the Genesis among them.

## Good to know

- **The installer updates itself.** From 1.0.6, ZyperOS Installer on macOS and Windows offers the next release and restarts into it. An installer up to 1.0.5 still opens the release page for its update.
- **Not included, add them if you want:** BIOS files, which go in `Bios/`. The README lists each one.
- **Your own games only.** ZyperOS comes with no games.
