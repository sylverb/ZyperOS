ZyperOS 1.1.0 is for the GKD Pixel 2 and the TrimUI Brick. It puts every download of game data on one page, and adds cheats, collections of your own, hotkeys you choose, a home for your screenshots, a volume boost past the top, an equalizer, new looks for MP4 and borders round handheld games. On the Pixel 2, the PICO-8 you bought can play the carts, with Splore, and the PSP and Saturn run faster. On the Brick, the Dreamcast, Nintendo 64, PSP and Saturn arrive, with the old-screen shaders, Screen Colors, Night Mode and Flash Drive.

## Install

1. Download **ZyperOS Installer** for your computer below: macOS (`.zip`), Windows (`.exe`), or Linux (`.deb` or `.rpm`).
2. Put the microSD card in the computer, open the installer, and pick your handheld (the GKD Pixel 2 or the TrimUI Brick), the latest release and the card. **Fresh install** erases the card and puts ZyperOS on it. On the Pixel 2, the installer's **PICO-8** group can add the PICO-8 for Raspberry Pi zip you bought (below).
3. Put the card in the handheld and turn it on. The first start sets the card up (about a minute), then asks for your language, and Zyper shows you each button.

For the Pixel 2, you can also write `ZyperOS-v1.1.0-pixel2.img.xz` with any disk image writer (Raspberry Pi Imager, balenaEtcher). Check it against `SHA256SUMS` first. The Brick needs the installer, which adds its closed files to the card (below).

## What's new

- **Your own PICO-8:** on the Pixel 2, the PICO-8 you bought from Lexaloffle can now play the PICO-8 shelf, every cart as PICO-8 plays it, with **Splore**, its online cart browser, first on the shelf. Add the PICO-8 for Raspberry Pi zip with ZyperOS Installer, or copy it to `Bios/PICO-8` on the card. ZyperOS never ships or downloads PICO-8. Your hotkeys, buttons, display modes, shaders and borders work over it, and MP4 plays on. Asked for on Discord.
- **Every download in one place:** **MENU > Downloads** is one page for covers, video snaps, names and details, achievements and cheats. **A**, **A** gets every kind at once, the servers side by side, and the page says how much and how long first. **B** leaves it running behind the menus, and **Y** shows each system and what it still lacks. It takes the place of the Scraper and Achievements rows.
- **Cheats:** the in-game menu's Game tab has **Cheats**, a list from libretro's cheat list (**MENU > Downloads** gets the files of your games only) or from a `.cht` file of your own in `Cheats/<system>/`. Each cheat turns on and off for that game, and the shelf says how many are on. While a cheat is on, the game's achievements wait; signed in to RetroAchievements, the first cheat asks first.
- **Collections of your own:** press **X** on any game and choose **Add to Collection**, or **New Collection** to name a list. Your lists are a group on the home after Favorites and Recents, mixing systems, each game on its own core. Each one is a text file in `Collections/`, in MinUI's and NextUI's format, so a list from another handheld works as it is.
- **Hotkeys you choose:** **MENU > System > Hotkeys** gives five new actions SELECT and a button of your choice: **Screenshot**, **Next Shader**, **Next Display**, **Reset Game** and **Quit**, for every game, one system or one game. Each starts as None. Quick Save, Quick Load, Faster and Slower keep their keys. A screenshot sounds a shutter as it is taken, with a short buzz on the Pixel 2.
- **Your screenshots on the home:** once you take a shot, the home has a **Screenshots** group, every shot newest first, with its game's cover in a corner. **A** opens a shot on the whole screen with nothing over it, Left and Right step through them, L1 and R1 jump to another game's, and **X** deletes one. OpenBOR's own screenshots are there too.
- **Louder than the top:** some films and games are quiet even at full volume. At the top, hold **VOL+** a second more, or press it three more times: up to six boost cells open, 3 dB each, up to +18 dB, with a limiter so nothing clips, and the top three also compress, so a loud game gets louder too. It works in games, MP4 and ports, and the brightness key lifts a dark picture the same way.
- **An equalizer:** **MENU > Sound > Equalizer** shapes what you hear in MP4, the menu music and games (on the Pixel 2, ports too): **Bass**, **Voice**, **Treble**, or five bands of your own. The speaker and headphones each keep their own preset, and a change is heard at once while music plays. It starts **Off**, and Off changes nothing.
- **MP4's new looks:** **Player Look** in MP4's MENU shows a song as its cover, as a **Cassette** whose reels turn with the song, as a record in its cover's color under a tonearm (**Vinyl**), with a big clock (**Classic**), or as bars that move to the music (**Spectrum**). MP4's shelf has Covers, Grid and List, which **START** turns. Videos get **Repeat** (Off, One or All), and MP4 comes back paused where it was after a power-off.
- **Borders round a handheld game:** a game's **Display** tab has **Border**, for the room around a handheld game: a picture of your own in `Borders/<system>/` (NextUI's overlays work too), the game blurred, or my drawing of the real Game Boy, Game Boy Color (in six colors) or Game Boy Advance around the game, at its scale. The Game Boy shelves' **Super Game Boy** row starts its games in their own colors and border.
- **A Video view, and Recents where you stopped:** **MENU > View** has a fourth choice, **Video**: the game in focus plays its video snap on the whole screen as you move. Recents can show each game's last screen instead of its cover, and says when you last played each game. A game on several discs shows once by its `.m3u`, and hidden systems keep their games on Favorites and Recents. Asked for on Discord.
- **Faster PSP and Saturn:** a PSP game that falls behind now leaves a late frame undrawn, as the DS does, and the Pixel 2's GPU runs on Mesa 26. On my Pixel 2, Tekken 6 went from 0.63x its speed to 0.96x, WipEout Pure from 0.47x to 0.67x, and games that kept up still do. Virtua Fighter 2 on the Saturn, reported on Discord, runs about 15% faster there, and no longer slows down after fast forward.

### Your library

- **Gambatte:** a second Game Boy and Game Boy Color core, on those shelves' **Core** row. mGBA stays the default.
- **More zips read:** zips packed with LZMA now play, and a zip ZyperOS can't read says so on the shelf.
- **A game on several discs, once:** an `.m3u` beside its discs is one game: the discs it lists leave the shelf, the Grid, Search Games and Random Game, and the `.m3u` starts the game, so disc swaps work as before. Asked for on Discord.
- **Hidden systems keep their games:** a system you turn off in **MENU > Display > Home Systems** leaves the home, Search Games and Random Game, and its games stay on Favorites and Recents, where they start, resume, and can be renamed or deleted. Asked for on Discord.
- **Folder names from other systems:** every ROM folder name that ROCKNIX, Knulli, Batocera, ArkOS, AmberELEC, JELOS, RetroPie and Recalbox use for a system ZyperOS has now reads on its shelf, `psx` and `playstation` alike. Asked by a player coming from ROCKNIX.
- **Five covers:** the Covers view shows five covers, the four beside the one in focus turned as MP4's albums are.
- **The home's Grid sizes itself:** with fewer systems on the home, the Grid's tiles grow; a full card keeps the six across of 1.0.6. Asked for on Discord.
- **Highlight:** **MENU > Display > Theme > Highlight** picks how the game or system in focus is marked on the home and the shelves: **Bar** (it grows a little, a short bar under it), **Border**, **Lift** or **None**.
- **Appearance Auto is back:** **MENU > Display > Appearance** has **Auto** again. It turns dark by Night Mode's Night From and Until, so one schedule does both. Asked for on Discord.
- **The game's file in its details:** **X** on a game shows **File**, its name as it is on the card with every bracket in it (`Final Fantasy VII (USA) (Disc 1)`), and for a game on several discs, how many. The shelves still show the short name. Asked for on Discord.
- **A core that won't load says why:** the shelf names the file and the reason, such as a core built for another kind of computer, where the game went back to the shelf with nothing said. Reported on Discord.
- **Famicom Disk System:** an `.fds` game says **Needs disksys.rom in Bios** on the shelf, where it went back with nothing said, and a pop-up says the disk's side when **L1** turns it.

### In a game

- **Screen Colors in a game:** the in-game menu's Display tab has a **Screen Colors** row, which opens MENU's page over the paused game, so you can fix a game's colors or contrast while you see it. Asked for on Discord.
- **Square Pixels:** a new Display mode, every pixel of the game square and the picture as tall as the screen.
- **Stick On/Off and Stick While Held:** on the Nintendo 64, Dreamcast, PlayStation and PSP, any button but SELECT can turn the d-pad into the stick, at each press or while held. The PSP's R2 does the first, as before. Asked for on Discord.
- **Two buttons on one:** in a button's list, **Y** on a choice another button already gives makes both give it, such as A and Y both as the NES's B; **A** swaps them, as before.
- **The handheld's own buttons:** MENU > Controls and a game's Controls pages draw each button as the handheld prints it: the Pixel 2's round SE and ST keys, the Brick's own keys, and the d-pad's arms.
- **The in-game menu over a program of its own:** over your PICO-8, OpenBOR, a PortMaster port, PortMaster itself and the standalone Flycast, the in-game menu has a game's tabs, with what that program can do, Screen Colors among them. **X** quits at once.
- **A quick press counts in a slow game:** a short press of MENU or of a SELECT hotkey is no longer lost in a game that draws few frames a second.
- **On-Screen Stats' Speed:** Speed is the game's own speed now, so a game that runs at 30 frames a second at full speed reads 1.0x, not 0.5x.
- **The Saturn's buttons:** C is now on R1 and Z on L1, as on the standalone emulators, so Virtua Fighter 2 kicks. Reported on Discord.
- **Dreamcast achievements:** Dreamcast games now get their achievements, and an achievement's card shows its badge and its whole description.

### MP4

- **AV1 videos:** MP4 plays AV1 videos now, which YouTube serves for many videos, and says so when a video is too big for the handheld's CPU, where it showed a slide show or a black box.
- **Big videos:** a 1440x1080 video no longer crashes MP4, or plays with a green line at its foot, on the Pixel 2. Reported on Discord.
- **The video player:** a long title slides, the picture grows to the whole screen on black when the controls go, Play sits in the middle, and MP4's MENU has **Quit**.
- **MP4 behind a game:** a song paused by a power-off is still paused under the game you come back to, the volume keys work on MP4's boost from the home and the menus, and a boost step in a game is heard in the music at once.

### Fixes

- **Held directions:** a held direction no longer steps back the other way on the home's Grid and Spotlight, in the save slots, in File Transfer's question and in the USB questions.
- **The home keeps up:** a step onto a system on the home no longer reads that system's covers from the card on the same frame, which made the Pixel 2's home stall (one frame took 416 ms) as the cursor crossed systems.
- **A core you add yourself** is closed after each game, so its next game starts clean. FreeIntv showed the last game frozen under the next one.
- **File Transfer:** the PIN's first box has the cursor as the page comes up, and the page shows each game's cover and video as the shelf finds them, on a card set up the EmulationStation way too. From the Discord.
- **Downloads keep what came:** a download left with **B** shows what it brought when you open the page again, and a game's video counts as on the card only when it is there.
- **Saturn discs:** a Saturn `.chd` game's files are closed when the game ends, so a game you delete frees its room on the card at once.
- **Side Lights:** **All on** now means every part, Battery Level too; a new card reads **5 on**.

## On the Pixel 2

- **Your PICO-8, in full:** Splore lists and downloads carts over Wi-Fi (with Wi-Fi off it asks first, or opens the carts it downloaded before). The shelf's Display modes, Shader, Strength and Border go over PICO-8, MENU in a cart has Display and On-Screen Stats, and your hotkeys, Reset, your own buttons and Auto Off work as in a game. MP4 plays on over it (PICO-8 goes quiet while it does), **MENU** then **SELECT** opens MP4, PICO-8's sound reaches a USB-C adapter, and the boost works over it. Carts count in Recents and Play Time, Splore's too.
- **Play time for programs of their own:** OpenBOR, the standalone Flycast and the Ports shelf's ports count in Recents and Play Time now, as your PICO-8 does.
- **PSP:** the late frame is left undrawn only while a game is behind, so a game that keeps up draws every frame: Daxter stays at 30 frames a second. God of War: Chains of Olympus went from 0.31x to 0.46x and Gran Turismo from 0.50x to 0.63x on my Pixel 2.
- **Mesa 26:** the Pixel 2's GPU driver is Mesa 26.2.6, where it was 25.0.7. Tekken 6 runs 12% faster on it, with none of the GPU faults the old one logged, and the N64, Dreamcast, Saturn and the shaders draw as before.
- **Saturn:** Virtua Fighter 2 went from 0.30x to 0.35x on my Pixel 2, and fast forward no longer leaves the frame skip off until the next game.
- **Battery health, measured:** **MENU > About** said **Battery health 100%** on every battery, because the Pixel 2 never measured it. It measures it now, after a full charge and a start at least half an hour later, at 50% or less, and the level counts from what your battery really holds. Until then About says **Not measured yet**.
- **A worn battery saves before it cuts out:** near empty, the level follows the voltage when the count reads 10 points or more too high, so the game saves and the handheld turns off at 2% before the battery cuts out under it. Reported on Discord.
- **The level while off:** on some Pixel 2s, a start from POWER read as a start on a charger, so the level kept falling while the handheld was off (30% in four hours for one player). The level now comes from the voltage after half an hour off, as it should. From a player's logs.
- **Ports that need Weston:** PortMaster's ports on its Westonpack runtime, 88 of them, Godot's and Java's among them, start now: the system has libevdev and libbz2. Reported on Discord. Ports' sound goes through the equalizer and the boost too.
- **Rumble, how strong:** **MENU > Sound > Rumble** is Off, Low, Medium or Full, and a game's Controls tab has a Rumble row for that game or its system. Each step buzzes at its level.

## On the TrimUI Brick

New on the Brick in 1.1.0:

- **The Dreamcast, Nintendo 64, PSP and Saturn:** the consoles whose cores draw with the GPU have their shelves on the Brick now, NAOMI and Atomiswave too, drawn by the Brick's own graphics chip. Testers ran them in the Brick's test kits; how fast each game runs on a Brick, your logs will tell me.
- **The old screens:** CRT, CRT Curved, Scanlines, LCD Grid and LCD No Backlight, in a game's Display tab and a system's menu, and on ZyperOS's own screens from **MENU > Display > Shader**. Border's Blur too.
- **Screen Colors and Night Mode:** **MENU > Display > Screen Colors** and **Night Mode**, as on the Pixel 2, in the display's own color table, so no game slows down for them.
- **Flash Drive, and a cable that asks:** on a computer's cable the Brick asks once what it is for, **Charge Only**, **File Transfer** or **Flash Drive**, which shows the card as a drive on the computer. On a charger it just charges.
- **On-Screen Stats in games,** and **Timing: Smooth** locked to the Brick's own screen.
- Everything in What's new above that does not say "on the Pixel 2".

Not on the Brick yet: PortMaster's ports, your own PICO-8 (FAKE-08 plays the carts), rumble in games, NES Player 2, the charge limit and the Brick's lights. Nobody has tried Bluetooth headphones with ZyperOS on a Brick yet. If something looks wrong on yours, your logs on the Discord help a lot: File Transfer's **Download logs**, or the card's `.userdata/brick/logs` folder.

## Updating from 1.0.6

On both handhelds, **MENU > System Update** offers 1.1.0 over Wi-Fi, the system and ZyperOS together, or use **Update** in ZyperOS Installer, which keeps your games, music and saves. Part of this release is in the system itself (on the Pixel 2: Mesa, the battery's measure and its empty end, the ports' libraries and their sound), and both ways install it. A Brick on a test kit's card is offered 1.1.0 too.

The game or system in focus now grows a little, with a short bar under it (**Highlight: Bar**). For a ring, a lift or no mark at all, pick another in **MENU > Display > Theme > Highlight**.

The Scraper's and Achievements' rows in MENU are now one row, **Downloads**.

**MENU > Display > Appearance** stays Light or Dark as 1.0.6 left it; pick **Auto** to have it follow the Night Hours again.

On the Brick, the Dreamcast, NAOMI, Atomiswave, Nintendo 64, PSP and Saturn shelves show from this update, with the games already in their folders.

On the Saturn, C is now on R1 and Z on L1. If you set the Saturn's shoulder buttons yourself, yours stay.

**MENU > Side Lights** reads **5 on** while Battery Level is off; **All on** now means every part.

On the Pixel 2, About says **Not measured yet** for the battery's health until the first measure.

## Good to know

- **The Simulator, without the build:** this release carries its QEMU image, `ZyperOS-v1.1.0-qemu.tar.xz`, with its sources tar. `tools/qemu-run.sh fetch` downloads it and checks it against `SHA256SUMS`, so the ZyperOS Simulator runs on a Mac with Apple silicon without building plastron. `make desk` runs the launcher itself in a window, as the Pixel 2 or the Brick ([Try it on your Mac](https://github.com/tarikbc/ZyperOS#try-it-on-your-mac)).
- **Not included, add them if you want:** BIOS files, which go in `Bios/`, and PICO-8, which is Lexaloffle's to sell. The guide lists each BIOS file.
- **Your own games only.** ZyperOS comes with no games.
