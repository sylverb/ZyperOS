ZyperOS Sound Ideas Lab

Three ideas from the Discord, as the handheld would play them: a Rumble level in MENU and in a game, an equalizer you hear as you tune it, and four new looks for MP4's Now Playing. Play them on live screens, then pick what is still open.

Connecting…

Decided already (your review of 2026-11-18). No row shows a "new" tag. Rumble is a row in MENU > Sound and in a game's Controls tab, on the Pixel 2 and the Brick (its motor is on GPIO 227, powered by the axp2202's bldo3 rail); Full is the default. Equalizer: you hear every step live while MP4 plays (MP4 changes the gains in its running FFmpeg graph and keeps about 60 ms queued while the page is open). Player Look: Cover stays the default; Cassette, Vinyl, Classic and Spectrum join it, animated, with bars drawn from the sound.
Click a screen, then ← ↑ → ↓, Enter A, Backspace B, Q L1, E R1, Space START. Hold a direction to repeat.
