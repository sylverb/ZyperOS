# MENU > Display > Screen Colors (src/panelgamma.h, DESIGN.md 11.42): colors
# for the whole screen, games and the shelf alike, made by the display's own
# table and its BCSH block. Opt-in: Standard changes nothing. The looks are
# named choices: capitals as each language writes a choice (GLOSSARY.md).
# The rows' values are signed numbers, -10 to +10, with no words of their own.
